# Artificial Intelligence Notes

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

## What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning is a subset of AI where computers learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

## What is Deep Learning?

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers.

## Difference Between AI, ML and Deep Learning

- AI is the broader concept of machines performing intelligent tasks.
- ML is a subset of AI that learns from data.
- Deep Learning is a subset of ML based on multi-layer neural networks.

## Applications of AI

- Chatbots
- Self-driving vehicles
- Face recognition
- Recommendation systems
- Medical diagnosis
- Voice assistants
- Fraud detection
